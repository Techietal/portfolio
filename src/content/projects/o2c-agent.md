## What it does
An AI-assisted workflow automation platform covering the full order-to-cash cycle: customer onboarding, KYC handling, order processing, invoice tracking, dispute management, email intake and human-in-the-loop escalation.

## What I built
- Operational workflow modules that achieve **90% automated order processing** across key workflow stages.
- REST APIs and PostgreSQL data models with **JWT authentication** and **RBAC-secured endpoints**.
- Background processing with **Celery jobs** and **Redis**, packaged as a **Dockerised** deployment.
